- REQ-007 (3) (`paths` is `{}`) is superseded from TASK-038 on; the export stays deterministic (REQ-007 (1), (2), (4), (5)).
- REQ-008 (2) is narrowed again: `serve` (TASK-045) and `users create-admin`, `users create-api-token`, `users reset-totp` (TASK-046) become real commands. `bits rebuild` and unknown commands keep the M0 behaviour.
- REQ-019: the serve profile gets its first consumer (TASK-045); its criteria are unchanged.
