# Requirements

Owner: planner. Ids are never renumbered or reused. Format per entry:
`REQ-### | title | spec ref | statement | acceptance criteria (observable, testable) | area`.

Spec line numbers (L…) refer to `docs/spec/Technical_Specification.md` as of 2026-10-19. "Planted" means a violation added in a throwaway copy or branch by the tester, never committed.
